The idea

Clay is more than material. It is livelihood, culture, and climate action in one form.

ClayBay gives skilled artisans a wider platform while offering people a practical alternative to plastic-heavy, mass-produced goods.

The brand begins with simple products, strong storytelling, and zero-loss onboarding for makers, then grows into a community around sustainable craft.

Idea person & founder

Meet Shiven Shukla.

Founder and idea person of The ClayBay, Shiven started this concept after observing both the struggles in society and the creative talent that often goes unnoticed.

His mission is simple and powerful: give artisans a platform, help their craft reach real markets, and build a brand where sustainable products also create social upliftment.
